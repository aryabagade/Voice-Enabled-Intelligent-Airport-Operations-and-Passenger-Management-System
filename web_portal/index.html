This system covers all 6 units of the Database Management Systems course syllabus. Explore the interactive modules below:

UNIT 1 CO1: Modeling

Data Modeling & Relational Integrity

3-Tier ANSI/SPARC architecture, ER modeling with Weak Entities, and Codd's 12 Rules.

  • Entities: Airport, Aircraft, Flight, Passenger, Booking, Payment, Baggage
  • Integrity: Entity PK, Referential FK CASCADE, CHECK (capacity > 0)
  • Codd's Rules: 100% compliant with Foundation & Information rules
UNITS 2 & 3 CO3: Relational SQL

Relational Algebra & Advanced SQL

Formal algebraic mappings (σ, π, ⨝, ρ, ∪, −) paired with equi/outer joins and correlated subqueries.

  • Complex Joins: Self joins for multi-leg layovers, Left outer joins for idle fleet
  • Views: v_flight_schedule, v_passenger_manifest, v_revenue_summary
  • Aggregates: GROUP BY with HAVING filters for high-revenue routes
UNIT 4 CO2 & CO4: Design

Normalization, Parquet & Recovery

1NF to BCNF decomposition proofs, columnar Parquet scan savings, and ARIES crash recovery.

  • Normal Forms: Lossless join & dependency preservation proven for 3NF/BCNF
  • Parquet vs Row: 10x I/O reduction via column-chunk & dictionary encoding
  • Recovery: Write-Ahead Logging (WAL) and checkpoint undo/redo protocol
UNIT 5 CO4 & CO5: Concurrency

Transactions, ACID & Concurrency

Strict 2-Phase Locking (2PL), Precedence Graph Conflict Serializability, and distributed sharding.

  • ACID Simulation: sp_book_flight with SAVEPOINT, seat lock, and ROLLBACK
  • Conflict Serializability: Serialization graph cycle-detection engine
  • Distributed DBMS: Range/hash partitioning across BOM, DEL, and BLR hubs
UNIT 6 CO6: Big Data

NoSQL, HDFS & Data Warehousing

Multi-model NoSQL storage (MongoDB, Redis, Cassandra, Neo4j) and OLAP Star Schema.

  • NoSQL Stores: JSON manifest documents, Redis radar cache, Neo4j flight graphs
  • CAP & BASE: Eventual consistency trade-off vs relational ACID
  • OLAP Star Schema: Fact_Flight_Operations surrounded by Time, Airport & Aircraft
EVALUATION Viva Preparation

Academic Defense & Evaluation

Ready for external DBMS oral evaluation, laboratory presentation, and viva voce questioning.

  • Auditing: Automatic trigger logging into flight_audit table
  • Dual-Backend: Seamless auto-detection between MySQL and SQLite
  • Voice Pipeline: Zero-latency Web Speech API + conversational response
